Keeping your account yours

Premium (Java / Microsoft)

Run /premium once to link your UUID. Future logins are automatic.

Cracked / offline

You must /login every session. Anyone can use your username, so use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Staff can't recover lost passwords on an anarchy server. Write yours down somewhere safe.

Supported versions
